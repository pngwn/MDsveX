The text these plugins collect has the smart punctuation of the compile. With `smart_punctuation` on, the `text` of a heading in `@mdsvex/plugin-toc` and the `title` and `excerpt` of `@mdsvex/plugin-page-data` have the quotes, dashes and ellipses the page shows. Both plugins now need mdsvex 1.1.
